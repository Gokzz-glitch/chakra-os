CHAKRA Privacy & DPDP Act 2023 Notice

Demo identifiers. Hospital UHID and ABHA examples on the cohort map are fictional. Use only synthetic patient details in this demo.

Encryption. Browser connections use HTTPS. Google Cloud Storage encrypts stored objects at rest.

Access. The research cohort and its run summaries are available to demo viewers. Real patient use requires authenticated clinical roles and hospital-specific access checks.

Gemini run guides. The saved guides explain boxes and masks. Their generation used model descriptions and excluded patient identifiers, videos and clinical notes.

Terms of Use — Non-SaMD Research Prototype

1. CDSCO Class B Scope: CHAKRA V2 is an investigational software prototype designed for clinical research and workflow augmentation. It does not replace clinician judgment.

2. Non-Diagnostic CADe Positioning: Real-time bounding box annotations generated by NVIDIA L4 workers represent visual prompts only. The system does not assert definitive histological diagnosis or optical biopsy classification.

3. Histopathology Mandate: All polyp detections remain provisionally labeled as PENDING_HISTOPATHOLOGY until formalin-fixed specimen evaluation is completed and signed off.

4. Attested Sign-Off: The attending gastroenterologist maintains sole legal and clinical accountability for all recorded Boston Bowel Prep Scale (BBPS) scores and surveillance intervals.
